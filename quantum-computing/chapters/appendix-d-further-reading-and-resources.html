Beginner-friendly books

BookAuthorWhy read it

Q is for QuantumTerry RudolphA brilliant no-math introduction to quantum weirdness
Dancing with QubitsRobert SutorBuilds the math from scratch

Free lecture notes and courses

  • John Preskill: Caltech Ph219 lecture notes on quantum computation
  • Ronald de Wolf: "Quantum Computing: Lecture Notes" (arXiv)
  • Andrew Childs: "Lecture Notes on Quantum Algorithms"
  • Scott Aaronson: "Introduction to Quantum Information Science" notes
  • Daniel Gottesman: PhD thesis, "Stabilizer Codes and Quantum Error Correction" (1997)
  • IBM Quantum Learning: free courses from basics to advanced, with Qiskit
  • PennyLane Codebook and demos: interactive QML and algorithm tutorials
  • Microsoft Quantum Katas: self-paced Q# exercises
  • Quantum Country (Andy Matuschak & Michael Nielsen): an essay with built-in spaced repetition
  • MIT OpenCourseWare and edX: university-level quantum computing courses

Websites and communities

  • arXiv quant-ph: where new research appears first
  • Quantum Algorithm Zoo: a catalogue of quantum algorithms
  • Error Correction Zoo: a catalogue of error-correcting codes
  • Shtetl-Optimized (Scott Aaronson's blog): sharp commentary on claims and hype
  • Quantum Computing Stack Exchange: Q&A
  • Unitary Foundation: open-source quantum software community and grants
  • Qiskit, Cirq and PennyLane community forums and GitHub repositories
