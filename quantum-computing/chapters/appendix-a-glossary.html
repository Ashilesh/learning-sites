The hookEvery term from Amplitude to W state. Search them, or flip through them as flashcards.

⚡ In 30 seconds (no jargon, promise)

Every term used in this guide, in one place. Type in the search box to filter, or switch to flashcard mode to test yourself.

Amplitude
A complex number attached to each basis state; its squared magnitude is the probability of that outcome
Ancilla
A helper qubit used for scratch work or error-syndrome measurement
Anyon
An exotic quasiparticle in 2-D systems; non-abelian anyons are the basis of topological qubits
Basis
A set of orthogonal states used to describe any state, e.g. {|0⟩, |1⟩} or {|+⟩, |−⟩}
Bell state
One of four maximally entangled two-qubit states, e.g. (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2
Bloch sphere
A sphere whose surface represents all pure states of one qubit
Born rule
Probability of an outcome = squared magnitude of its amplitude
BQP
The class of problems efficiently solvable by a quantum computer
Bra / Ket
⟨ψ| (row vector) and |ψ⟩ (column vector) in Dirac notation
Circuit depth
Number of sequential layers of gates in a circuit
Clifford gates
Gates generated by H, S and CNOT; efficiently simulable classically (Gottesman–Knill)
CNOT (CX)
Two-qubit gate that flips the target qubit if the control is |1⟩
Coherence time
How long a qubit retains its quantum state (T₁, T₂)
Collapse
The update of a quantum state to the observed outcome upon measurement
Complementarity
Certainty in one basis implies total uncertainty in a complementary basis
CRQC
"Cryptographically relevant quantum computer": one able to break RSA/ECC
Decoherence
Loss of quantum behaviour due to interaction with the environment
Decoder
Classical algorithm that infers errors from syndrome measurements
Density matrix
A matrix ρ describing pure or mixed (noisy, uncertain) quantum states
Dequantisation
Finding a classical algorithm that matches a claimed quantum speedup
Dilution refrigerator
Cryostat reaching ~10 mK, used for superconducting qubits
Distance (code)
Minimum number of physical errors that can cause an undetected logical error
Dynamic circuit
Circuit with mid-circuit measurement and classically controlled operations
Entanglement
Correlation between quantum systems that can't be described by each system separately
Error mitigation
Statistical techniques that reduce noise in averaged results without full error correction
Expectation value
The average measured value of an observable: ⟨ψ|O|ψ⟩
Fault tolerance
Computing reliably with faulty components by using error correction throughout
Fidelity
A measure of closeness between quantum states or operations (1 = perfect)
Gate
A reversible (unitary) operation on qubits
GHZ state
Multi-qubit entangled state (|00…0⟩ + |11…1⟩)/√2
Hadamard (H)
Gate that creates equal superpositions: |0⟩ → |+⟩, |1⟩ → |−⟩
Hamiltonian
The operator describing a system's energy and its time evolution
Hilbert space
The mathematical space of quantum states (dimension 2ⁿ for n qubits)
Interference
Amplitudes adding constructively or cancelling destructively
Josephson junction
Thin insulating barrier between superconductors; the non-linear element in superconducting qubits
Logical qubit
A qubit encoded across many physical qubits with error correction
Magic state
A special resource state enabling non-Clifford gates (like T) in fault-tolerant computers
Measurement
Extracting a classical result from a quantum state, which disturbs the state
Mixed state
A probabilistic mixture of quantum states, describing noise or ignorance
NISQ
Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum: today's pre-fault-tolerant era
No-cloning theorem
An unknown quantum state cannot be copied
Observable
A measurable quantity, represented by a Hermitian operator
Oracle
A black-box subroutine that computes a function, used in query-complexity analyses
Pauli gates
X (bit flip), Y, Z (phase flip)
Phase
The complex angle of an amplitude; relative phase is physically meaningful
Phase kickback
When a controlled operation writes information into the phase of the control qubit
Physical qubit
An actual hardware qubit (as opposed to a logical qubit)
PQC
Post-quantum cryptography: classical algorithms designed to resist quantum attacks
QAOA
Quantum Approximate Optimisation Algorithm
QEC
Quantum error correction
QFT
Quantum Fourier Transform
QKD
Quantum key distribution (e.g. BB84)
qLDPC code
Quantum low-density parity-check code; efficient codes with sparse checks
QPE
Quantum phase estimation
QPU
Quantum processing unit
Quantum advantage
Outperforming classical computers on a (usually useful) task
Quantum supremacy
Outperforming classical computers on any task, even a useless one
Quantum volume
IBM's benchmark of the largest square random circuit a machine can run reliably
Qubit
Quantum bit: a two-level quantum system
Qudit
A d-level quantum system (generalisation of a qubit)
Rydberg atom
An atom with an electron in a very high energy level, used for neutral-atom gates
Shot
One run of a quantum circuit ending in measurement
Stabilizer
A multi-qubit Pauli operator whose measurement detects errors without disturbing encoded data
Superconducting qubit
A qubit made from a superconducting circuit (e.g. a transmon)
Superposition
A combination of basis states with complex amplitudes
Surface code
A 2-D topological error-correcting code with ~1% threshold
Syndrome
The pattern of stabilizer measurement results that reveals errors
T gate
The π/4 phase gate; with Clifford gates it gives universality
T₁ / T₂
Energy relaxation time / phase coherence time
Teleportation
Transferring a quantum state using entanglement plus two classical bits
Tensor product (⊗)
The operation combining state spaces of multiple systems
Threshold
The physical error rate below which error correction improves with code size
Topological qubit
A qubit protected by global (topological) properties of exotic matter
Transmon
The most common superconducting qubit design
Transpilation
Compiling a circuit to a specific hardware's gates and connectivity
Trotterisation
Approximating evolution under a sum of Hamiltonians by alternating small steps
Unitary
A matrix with U†U = I; every quantum gate is unitary
VQE
Variational Quantum Eigensolver
W state
Entangled state (|001⟩ + |010⟩ + |100⟩)/√3, robust to qubit loss
