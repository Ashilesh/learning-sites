1.1

🤔What is System Design?

System design is planning all the pieces of a big computer system, how they connect, and what happens when lots of people show up or something breaks.

👶 Like you're 5

You open a lemonade stand 🍋. One friend comes — easy! Now the whole city wants lemonade. You need more helpers, more lemons, a line system, a fridge so you don't squeeze every lemon fresh, and a backup plan if a helper gets sick. Planning all of that is system design.

🧱 Pieces
Servers, databases, caches, queues… the LEGO bricks.
🔌 Connections
Who talks to whom, and in what language (APIs).
🌪️ What-ifs
10× more users? A server catches fire? A whole country goes offline?
⚖️ Choices
Every choice has a cost. Good design = good trade-offs.

📨What happens when you open a website?

Let's follow one request — say you open pizza.com — through the whole system. Press Play or go step by step.

📖 DNS — the internet's phone book: name → number.
🚚 CDN — copies of pictures stored near you.
